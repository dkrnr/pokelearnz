Microphone and online questions

After you allow this, tapping the mic records audio, then sends it for transcription and an answer. Native browser microphone permission is still required. You can withdraw this permission here.

Questions, voice and actual providers

Netlify hosts the app and proxies requests. Audio goes to Valsea (api.valsea.ai) for transcription. Questions and voice transcripts go to OpenRouter (openrouter.ai). Configured models: Google Gemma, NVIDIA Nemotron, InclusionAI Ling, and the OpenRouter free-router fallback. OpenRouter chooses the downstream inference host; this app cannot identify that host or control provider retention. No sentiment requests are sent.

Questions, transcripts, recordings and answers are never saved or cached by this app. No browser speech recognition service is used. Optional read-aloud uses local device speechSynthesis voices; if unavailable, captions continue silently.

Sprite hosts and offline use

Pokémon images load from cdn.jsdelivr.net (jsDelivr CDN for PokeAPI sprites). That host receives image requests and your IP address, never questions or recordings. Visited sprites are cached on this device, with a 180-image limit. All 1,025 names and types work offline after first load; unvisited pictures may need internet. No full image collection is bundled.

Optional device saving

Permission setup is saved separately so it is only needed once. Recent buddies stay temporary unless you enable saving. No rewards or progress counters.

Language

New Sinhala and Tamil interface strings are machine-translated drafts awaiting human review. Buddy names and scientific answers remain in English. Transcription follows the selected language.
