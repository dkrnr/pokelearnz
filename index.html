Questions, voice and actual providers

Last request · debug

Netlify hosts the app and proxies requests. Audio goes to Valsea (api.valsea.ai) for transcription. Questions and voice transcripts go to OpenRouter (openrouter.ai) and its selected model host. Development uses an ordered, configurable list of free models; no free-router. Requests inherit the developer’s OpenRouter account privacy options. Free AI providers may retain or train on questions depending on those options and their policies. This app does not guarantee zero retention. A private or paid provider must be reviewed before public launch. When online answers are unavailable, common questions can use authored answers.

Valsea’s public policy states 30-day default retention, encryption in transit and at rest, sharing with necessary cloud and speech/AI sub-processors, and no use of audio to train its models without explicit written consent. Different account retention schedules may apply. We have not audited this account’s actual handling. Its terms require account users to be at least 13; suitability and consent for a service used by ages 6–9 need provider clarification before launch. See the privacy document in this repository for the policy source and remaining review.

This app does not persist or cache questions, transcripts, recordings or answers. Netlify and the providers process them; their logging/retention policies still need review. Server quotas store counts and daily salted IP hashes in Netlify Blobs; cleanup attempts remove old counters on later activity, with no automatic deletion guarantee. The simple device consent gate is not authenticated proof of parental consent. Private details detected in text are blocked before OpenRouter, but recordings may already contain them when sent to Valsea. No browser speech recognition service is used. Authored activities support prerecorded files served by this app (none recorded yet). Optional read-aloud falls back to local device speechSynthesis voices; if unavailable, captions continue silently.

Sprite hosts and offline use

Pokémon images load from cdn.jsdelivr.net (jsDelivr CDN for PokeAPI sprites). That host receives image requests and your IP address, never questions or recordings. Visited sprites are cached on this device, within a 12 MiB byte budget (images over 1 MiB are not cached). All 1,025 names and types work offline after first load; unvisited pictures may need internet. No full image collection is bundled.

Optional device saving

Permission setup is saved separately so it is only needed once. Recent buddies stay temporary unless you enable saving. No rewards or progress counters.

Language

New Sinhala and Tamil interface strings are machine-translated drafts awaiting human review. Buddy names and scientific answers remain in English. Transcription follows the selected language.
