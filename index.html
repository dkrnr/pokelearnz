Questions and privacy

About · Parents · Privacy · Contact

Last request · debug

This is a university demo. Children should not enter personal information. Audio goes to Valsea. Authored answers and a output-checked server cache answer known questions before Groq and OpenRouter free models. Free AI providers may retain or train on questions. See how it works and its safety limits and the full data flow and provider policies.

The app stores no question, transcript or recording history. It stores a small server cache of screened answers, private question hashes and request-limit counts. Cached model answers are unverified and may be wrong. They stop serving after 24 hours; cleanup happens on later activity. The arithmetic gate is not authenticated parental consent; audio may contain private speech before text checks. Clear device data cannot delete server/provider records.

Buddy pictures and offline use

Pokémon images load from cdn.jsdelivr.net (a public picture service). That host receives image requests and your IP address, never questions or recordings. Visited sprites are cached on this device, within a small storage limit. All 1,025 names and types work offline after first load; unvisited pictures may need internet. No full image collection is bundled.

Optional device saving

Permission setup is saved separately so it is only needed once. Recent buddies stay temporary unless you enable saving. No rewards or progress counters.

Language

New Sinhala and Tamil interface strings are machine-translated drafts awaiting human review. Buddy names and scientific answers remain in English. Transcription follows the selected language.
