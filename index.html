For grown-ups

Six authored activities for ages 6–9. No timers, reward counters, notifications or chat continuation prompts. A child can stop at any time. AI answers are optional and can be inaccurate; explore them together.

Questions, recordings and providers

Hosting and function requests go to Netlify. Typed questions and transcribed voice text go to OpenRouter, which routes them to an inference provider. The configured models are Google Gemma, NVIDIA Nemotron and InclusionAI Ling, with an OpenRouter free-router fallback. The frontend cannot identify the actual downstream inference host; OpenRouter selects it. Provider retention is outside this app's control.

Voice recordings go to Valsea (api.valsea.ai) for transcription. Recording starts only after a tap and microphone permission. The transcript is shown for review; sending it to OpenRouter takes another tap. No sentiment requests are sent. Local read-aloud uses only installed browser voices marked local; recordings, questions and answers are never cached or saved by this app.

Optional device saving

Saving is off by default. You can save only the buddy choice and one completed-activity flag. No names, questions, transcripts, answers or audio are stored. App files are cached separately for offline use.

Buddy

The default buddy is an original character. Fan artwork can be replaced through one buddy module.

Unofficial fan project, not affiliated with Nintendo, Game Freak, Creatures or The Pokémon Company.

Language

Lessons and read-aloud are in English. Sinhala and Tamil drafts need human review. They are not presented as reviewed learning content.

Read-aloud and sounds

Listen uses speechSynthesis with a locally installed English voice. If none is available, instructions remain visible and a grown-up can read them. Sound effects start off each visit. The child can turn them on or off.

Install and updates

Use your browser's “Add to Home Screen” or “Install app” menu. Activities work offline after the first successful visit. Questions and transcription need internet. Updates wait until the child leaves an activity.
